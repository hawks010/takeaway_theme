What are we actually building?

Three connected systems — pick which ones belong in this project

Zone 1 — Reservations

Booking engine

  • Online booking form
  • Auto-confirm / pending flow
  • Optional deposit (via WooCommerce)
  • Confirmation & reminder emails
  • Staff admin: upcoming list, confirm / cancel / modify
  • CRM toggle (on/off per business)
Zone 2 — Live table service

Table management

  • Floor plan: tables with numbers/names
  • Seat a party: link reservation → table
  • Staff take orders per table
  • Orders sent to kitchen as tickets tagged by table
  • Bill calculation & payment per table
  • Table status: free / occupied / reserved / cleaning
Zone 3 — CRM

Customer records

  • Customer profile: name, phone, email, preferences
  • Booking history per customer
  • Order history per customer
  • Notes (allergies, VIP, regulars)
  • Linked to WooCommerce account
  • Marketing segments
The honest truth about scope: Zone 1 (reservations) is a focused, buildable system — weeks of work. Zone 2 (live table service) is essentially a lightweight POS — months of work, and a very different product. Zone 3 (CRM) plugs on top of either. Most restaurants start with Zone 1, then decide if they actually need Zone 2 (many don't — they use a notepad for table orders).
What you described: "a business using the website to log all table orders and assign against a table with a user's name" = Zone 2 (table management). The question is whether Zone 2 is worth building custom, or whether the real value is Zone 1 (reservations online → confirmed → customer shows up) and the kitchen ticket link is just a simple "tag this order with table/name".
